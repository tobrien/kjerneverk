## Key Messaging Established

### Core Message
"Kjerneverk provides structured formats for working with generative AI. It moves you from freeform prompts, unstructured plans, and documents generated without your input toward constructs that give you control, ownership, and tool independence."

### The Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
"Kjerneverk provides structured formats for the core work you do with generative AI. Instead of freeform prompts, unstructured plans, or documents generated without your input, you get constructs with tools and infrastructure that give you control. It's about making your work truly yours—not just pattern-matched from training data. And it works with any model, any tool, any environment."

### The One-Liner
"Structured formats for generative AI that give you control over your prompts, plans, and documents."

### Key Phrases Established
**Use these**:
- "Structured formats for generative AI"
- "The thing is bigger than just the thing"
- "Control and ownership"
- "Process over tools"
- "Tool independence"
- "Hits the mark, but not quite"
- "Truly yours, not pattern-matched"

**Avoid these**:
- "AI development tools"
- "AI-assisted software development"
- "Revolutionary" or "disruptive"
- "Easy AI" or "simple solutions"

## Tone Established

### Core Attributes
- **Pragmatic**: Solving real problems, not theoretical possibilities
- **Opinionated**: Strong views about how to work with generative AI
- **Process-Focused**: Emphasizing how you work, not just what you produce
- **Intellectually Honest**: Don't oversimplify, use proper technical terms

### Voice Characteristics
- **Direct**: Get to the point, don't bury the message
- **Specific**: Use concrete examples, name real tools
- **Thoughtful**: Explain why, not just what

## Target Audience Defined

**Primary**: Technical users working with generative AI who are:
- Closer to programming than not
- Doing complex work requiring thoughtful planning
- Frustrated with "good enough" outputs from LLMs
- Wanting more control over AI-assisted workflows

**Personas Created**:
1. The Thoughtful Developer (Alex) - Building AI-assisted tools
2. The Power User (Jordan) - Technical writer doing research
3. The AI-Assisted Worker (Sam) - Software engineer using Cursor
4. The Researcher (Morgan) - Academic/industry researcher

## Key Concepts Documented

### Constructs
Structured formats that encompass more than just artifacts—they include process, lifecycle, ways of thinking, and infrastructure.

### Tool Independence
The principle that your work should not be locked to a specific model, IDE, or platform. Works with any model or no model at all.

### Process Over Tools
As models improve, which tool you use matters less. What matters is your process and structure.

### Metacognitive Programming
Programming that thinks about programming. Prompting about prompting. Making your thinking process explicit and manageable.

### "The Thing Is Bigger Than Just The Thing"
The foundational philosophy: prompts, plans, and documents are not just artifacts—they're processes, lifecycles, and ways of thinking.

## Before and After Framing

### RiotPrompt
- **Before**: Freeform text blocks, rewritten for each model, no history
- **After**: Structured prompts that work everywhere, with evolution tracking

### RiotPlan
- **Before**: Tool-generated inadequacy, markdown chaos, Beads (issue tracking without deep thinking)
- **After**: Full lifecycle with analysis, shaping, and execution

### RiotDoc
- **Before**: Blank canvas or "close enough" LLM generation
- **After**: Construction of meaning with full history and process tracking

## Tool Independence Emphasized

All three constructs work:
- **From CLI** with API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini)
- **Via MCP** with any model attached to session
- **As libraries** in applications
- **With or without AI** - formats useful even without models
