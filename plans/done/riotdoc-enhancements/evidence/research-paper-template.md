# Research Paper Document Template

## Template Structure

This defines the structure and questions for creating research paper documents in RiotDoc.

---

## Questions to Answer

### Core Research
- **Research question**: What are you investigating?
- **Hypothesis**: What do you expect to find? (if applicable)
- **Field/discipline**: What area of study?
- **Target journal/venue**: Where will this be published?
- **Citation style**: APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE, etc.?

### Background
- **Literature review**: What existing research is relevant?
- **Research gap**: What's missing in current knowledge?
- **Significance**: Why does this matter?

### Methodology
- **Research design**: Qualitative, quantitative, mixed methods?
- **Data collection**: How will you gather data?
- **Analysis approach**: How will you analyze the data?
- **Limitations**: What are the constraints?

### Audience & Scope
- **Target audience**: Academic peers, practitioners, general public?
- **Length**: Short paper (3-5k words), standard (6-8k), extended (10k+)?
- **Sections required**: Does the venue require specific sections?

---

## Output Document Structure

```markdown
# [Paper Title]

**Author(s)**: [Names and affiliations]
**Date**: [Date]
**Keywords**: [keyword1, keyword2, keyword3]

---

## Abstract
[150-250 word summary of the entire paper: research question, methods, findings, conclusions]

---

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Background
[Context and background information]

### 1.2 Research Question
[Clear statement of what you're investigating]

### 1.3 Significance
[Why this research matters]

### 1.4 Paper Structure
[Brief overview of paper organization]

---

## 2. Literature Review
### 2.1 [Topic Area 1]
[Review of relevant literature]

### 2.2 [Topic Area 2]
[Review of relevant literature]

### 2.3 Research Gap
[What's missing that this paper addresses]

---

## 3. Methodology
### 3.1 Research Design
[Overall approach and justification]

### 3.2 Data Collection
[How data was gathered]

### 3.3 Analysis Methods
[How data was analyzed]

### 3.4 Limitations
[Constraints and boundaries]

---

## 4. Results
### 4.1 [Finding 1]
[Presentation of findings]

### 4.2 [Finding 2]
[Presentation of findings]

---

## 5. Discussion
### 5.1 Interpretation
[What the results mean]

### 5.2 Implications
[Theoretical and practical implications]

### 5.3 Comparison with Prior Research
[How findings relate to existing literature]

---

## 6. Conclusion
### 6.1 Summary
[Brief recap of research and findings]

### 6.2 Contributions
[What this adds to the field]

### 6.3 Future Research
[Questions for further investigation]

---

## References
[Citations in specified format]

---

## Appendices

[Supplementary material]
```
