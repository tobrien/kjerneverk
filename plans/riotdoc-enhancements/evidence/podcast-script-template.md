# Podcast Script Document Template

## Template Structure

This defines the structure and questions for creating podcast script documents in RiotDoc.

---

## Questions to Answer

### Idea/Draft Phase

#### Episode Basics
- **Episode title**: What's the episode called?
- **Episode number**: What number is this episode? (if part of a series)
- **Topic**: What's the main subject?
- **Angle**: What's your unique take or approach?
- **Target length**: Short (10-15 min), medium (20-30 min), long (45-60 min+)?

#### Format & Structure
- **Format**: Interview, solo, co-hosted, panel, narrative storytelling?
- **Segments**: What are the main segments? (intro, main content, Q&A, outro, ads, etc.)
- **Tone**: Conversational, educational, entertaining, serious, humorous?
- **Pacing**: Fast-paced, relaxed, contemplative?

#### Content Planning
- **Key points**: What are the 3-5 main points to cover?
- **Stories/examples**: What stories or examples will you use?
- **Guests**: Who will be on the episode? (if applicable)
- **Questions**: What questions will you ask? (for interviews)

#### Audio Elements
- **Music/sound**: Intro music, outro music, sound effects?
- **Transitions**: How will segments transition?

### Publishing Phase

#### Production Details
- **Show notes**: What should go in the show notes?
- **Timestamps**: Key moments to timestamp?
- **Links**: Resources, references, or links to include?
- **Transcript**: Will there be a transcript?

#### Distribution
- **Podcast feed**: Which podcast feed/show?
- **Release date**: When will this be published?
- **Social media**: How will this be promoted?
- **Episode description**: SEO-optimized description for podcast platforms?
- **Tags/categories**: Apple Podcasts categories, tags?

---

## Output Document Structure

```markdown
# Podcast Script: [Episode Title]

**Show**: [Podcast Name]
**Episode**: [Number]
**Length**: [Target length]
**Format**: [Format type]
**Date**: [Recording/Release date]

---

## Episode Overview

**Topic**: [Main topic]
**Angle**: [Unique perspective]
**Key Takeaways**:
- [Takeaway 1]
- [Takeaway 2]
- [Takeaway 3]

---

## Script

### [INTRO MUSIC - 0:00]

**[HOST]**: [Opening lines]

[Intro content - welcome, episode topic, what listeners will learn]

---

### [SEGMENT 1: Main Content - X:XX]

**[HOST]**: [Transition to main content]

[Main discussion points, stories, examples]

**[GUEST]** (if applicable): [Guest responses/contributions]

[Continue with structured conversation/narrative]

---

### [SEGMENT 2: Topic Name - X:XX]

**[HOST]**: [Transition]

[Content for this segment]

---

### [SEGMENT 3: Q&A / Discussion - X:XX]

**[HOST]**: [Questions or discussion points]

[Responses and elaboration]

---

### [OUTRO - X:XX]

**[HOST]**: [Summary of key points]

[Call to action - subscribe, review, visit website, etc.]

[Thank guests, credits]

### [OUTRO MUSIC - X:XX]
```
