# Project Plan Document Template

## Template Structure

This defines the structure and questions for creating project plan documents in RiotDoc.

---

## Questions to Answer

### Project Overview
- **Project name**: What's the project called?
- **Project goal**: What are you trying to achieve?
- **Stakeholders**: Who's involved? Who cares about this?
- **Timeline**: When does this need to be done?
- **Budget/resources**: What resources are available?

### Scope
- **In scope**: What's included in this project?
- **Out of scope**: What's explicitly NOT included?
- **Deliverables**: What will be produced?
- **Success criteria**: How will you know it's successful?

### Approach
- **Methodology**: Agile, waterfall, hybrid, custom?
- **Phases**: What are the major phases or milestones?
- **Dependencies**: What needs to happen first?
- **Risks**: What could go wrong?

### Team & Communication
- **Team structure**: Who's doing what?
- **Communication plan**: How will the team stay in sync?
- **Decision-making**: Who approves what?
- **Reporting**: How often and to whom?

---

## Output Document Structure

```markdown
# Project Plan: [Project Name]

## Executive Summary
[Brief overview of project, goal, timeline, and key deliverables]

## Project Overview
- **Goal**: [Primary goal]
- **Timeline**: [Start - End dates]
- **Budget**: [Budget or resource allocation]
- **Stakeholders**: [List of stakeholders]

## Scope
### In Scope
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]

### Out of Scope
- [Item 1]
- [Item 2]

### Deliverables
1. [Deliverable 1]
2. [Deliverable 2]

### Success Criteria
- [Criterion 1]
- [Criterion 2]

## Approach
### Methodology
[Description of approach]

### Phases & Milestones
1. **Phase 1**: [Name] - [Timeline]
   - Milestone: [Description]
2. **Phase 2**: [Name] - [Timeline]
   - Milestone: [Description]

### Dependencies
- [Dependency 1]
- [Dependency 2]

### Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|------------|
| [Risk 1] | [High/Med/Low] | [High/Med/Low] | [Strategy] |

## Team & Responsibilities
| Role | Person | Responsibilities |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| [Role] | [Name] | [Description] |

## Communication Plan
- **Team meetings**: [Frequency and format]
- **Status reports**: [Frequency and audience]
- **Decision log**: [Where decisions are recorded]

## Appendices
- [Supporting documents]
- [References]
```
